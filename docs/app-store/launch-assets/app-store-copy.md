## Description

Built for the field.

FieldSoli is a free job, time, and profit tracker for independent contractors and tradespeople. Track work time, material costs, job notes, revenue, and payments for every job, without a full field service management system to learn and maintain.

Job details get scattered. Time spent lives in one place, materials and costs in another, notes somewhere else, and a paid invoice still doesn’t tell you what the job actually earned. FieldSoli keeps the record together so you can see the work behind the number.

Track live work sessions, add materials and notes as the job takes shape, then record revenue and payments. Review open, incomplete, or unpaid jobs and use earnings, net hourly rate, and job comparisons to understand which work paid best.

Our mission is to empower independent tradespeople with the technology, data, and opportunities available to the largest companies, so they can compete, thrive, and build successful businesses on their own terms.

FieldSoli is free to use. If paid plans come later, we’ll explain what changes before anything does. Earnings are based on the information you enter and are not accounting or tax advice.
