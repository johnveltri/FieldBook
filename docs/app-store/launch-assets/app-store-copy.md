## Description

Built for the field.

FieldSoli is a free, focused job and earnings tracker for independent tradespeople. It is for the work you are already doing—not another full field service management (FSM) system to learn and maintain.

Job details have a way of getting scattered. Time lives in one place, materials & costs in another, notes somewhere else, and a paid invoice still does not tell you what the job actually earned. FieldSoli keeps the record together so you can see the work behind the number.

Track sessions live while the work happens. Add materials, notes, revenue, and payment as the job takes shape. Review work that is open, incomplete, or unpaid to include it in earnings reporting. Then use earnings, net hourly rate, and job comparisons to understand which work paid best.

FieldSoli is deliberately focused: a reliable job record and clearer earnings data, without the weight of a full dispatch, CRM, scheduling, or accounting platform.

Use FieldSoli at the pace of your work. Start a session in the field when you begin, add what belongs to the job in the moment, and return to complete the record when it is time to review earnings. The point is a clearer record—with less administrative work.

## OUR MISSION

Our mission is to empower independent tradespeople with the technology, data, and opportunities available to the largest companies, so they can compete, thrive, and build successful businesses on their own terms.

FieldSoli starts by giving independent tradespeople a reliable record of their work and clearer earnings data.

FieldSoli is free to use. If paid plans come later, we’ll explain what changes before anything does. Earnings are based on the information you enter and are not accounting or tax advice.
